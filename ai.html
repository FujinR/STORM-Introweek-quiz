Cheat sheet

  • Nearest neighbor: classify a new point the same way as the most similar point(s) already seen.
  • Probability from data: (cases matching the condition) ÷ (total cases).
  • Overfitting: the model memorizes training data instead of learning the general pattern — high training accuracy, low accuracy on new data.
  • Weighted sum: prediction = (weight₁ × input₁) + (weight₂ × input₂) + bias.
  • Confusion matrix: TP (true positive), FP (false positive), TN (true negative), FN (false negative). Accuracy = (TP+TN) ÷ total.

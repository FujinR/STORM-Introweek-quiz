Mika, a junior analyst, has three days before the board meeting to figure out why the company is bleeding money — and what to do about it.

Cheat sheet

  • Growth rate: (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100%.
  • Median: the middle value when sorted — less skewed by outliers than the mean.
  • ROI: (profit − cost) ÷ cost × 100%.
  • Churn rate: customers lost ÷ customers at the start of the period × 100%.
  • Correlation ≠ causation: two things moving together doesn't mean one causes the other.

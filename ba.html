Business Analytics

Turn numbers into a decision.

Five quick questions on reading data and making the call. Cheat sheet included for anything new.

Cheat sheet

  • Mean (average): add up all values, divide by how many there are.
  • Median: the middle value when all values are sorted — less affected by extreme outliers than the mean.
  • Correlation: when two things tend to move together. Correlation does NOT prove one causes the other.
  • ROI (Return on Investment): (profit − cost) ÷ cost, usually shown as a percentage.
  • Churn rate: the percentage of customers who stop using a product over a given period.

Mathematics

Logic, proof, and pattern.

Five quick questions on the reasoning tools that sit underneath every other faculty here. Cheat sheet included.

Cheat sheet

  • Prime number: a whole number greater than 1 with exactly two divisors — 1 and itself.
  • ∀ (for all) / ∃ (there exists): logic symbols meaning "for every case" / "at least one case exists".
  • Proof by contradiction: assume the opposite of what you want to prove, then show that assumption leads to something impossible.
  • Sequence: an ordered list of numbers following a rule, e.g. 2, 4, 6, 8 (add 2 each time).
  • Set: an unordered collection of distinct items, e.g. {1, 2, 3}.
